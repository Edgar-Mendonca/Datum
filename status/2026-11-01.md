# Datum family status: 2026-11-01

A dated snapshot of where each app stands, what it lacks, and where to put the effort next. Written in hub session 25 from shallow clones of all eight app repos (code size, tests, releases, docs, CI) and each app's `docs/site.json` roadmap and `docs/MEMORY.md` known limits. Write a new dated file for the next review; do not edit this one.

## Context

- Eight apps built in under two weeks (Plan started 2026-10-24; Schema 2026-10-29), working day and night.
- **Real users:** Datum **Plan**, **Plot** and **Calc** are in use by people outside the project. Plot is used by several scientific laboratories, which are reporting issues. The other five apps have not been used for real work yet.
- **Lesson:** real-world use finds problems that the tests and examples do not (Plan's real-world testing showed this first). Building faster than anyone uses the apps creates quality debt.
- **Decision (user):** keep the pace, but slow down enough to **validate every app with real-world use** before adding more features.

## Each app: progress (rough share of a mature commercial product's single-user capability), gaps, next

**1. Plan: ~65–70%.**
- **Gaps:** exchange files tested against real files from other tools; speed at 100,000 tasks; several people on one plan; delay analysis.
- **Next:** the 1.0 work, not more features.

**2. Plot: ~70% for lab and research work.**
- **Gaps:** a validation report against reference results (the public NIST statistical reference datasets); Mac and Linux builds.
- **Next:** work through the issues the labs report first, then the validation report.

**3. Mesh: ~75% for frames and 2D parts, ~40% for general 3D.**
- **Gaps:**
  - a geometry pipeline (CAD solids in, own meshing);
  - contact and brick elements;
  - testing on files from real meshing programs ("not yet tried on files from a real mesher").
- **Next:** depth over breadth.

**4. Pipe: ~55–60% of a static pipe stress tool; the most relevant to the user's own field.**
- **Gaps:** dynamics; other piping codes; the pump standard's combined nozzle equations; comparison with real projects.
- **Next:** rerun two or three of the user's past (non-confidential) stress models and compare with their recorded results; then dynamics.

**5. Schema: ~45% of the enterprise class.**
- **Gaps:** DEXPI tested with files from other programs; large projects (100+ sheets); real drawings; multi-user.
- **Next:** harden before adding the rest of the roadmap.

**6. Sim: ~30%; stopped since 2026-10-30.**
- **Gaps:**
  - control design on whole models (linearise, root locus, Nyquist, PID auto-tuning);
  - physical (acausal) modelling, algebraic loops, FMU;
  - the motor library not yet run on a Windows PC.
- **Next:** restart with 0.3 control design.

**7. Calc: ~30%; the smallest, and in use.**
- **Gaps:** matrices, symbolic maths, programming (loops, conditions), ODEs, a more comfortable equation editor.
- **Next:** fix what its users report; then matrices, then programming.

**8. Atlas: ~25–30%; least progressed and blocked.**
- **Gaps:** only 73 of ~250 materials' values checked against published datasheets; Mesh, Pipe and Calc take their materials from Atlas, so this weakens them too.
- **Blocked:** waiting on the user's answers to the six questions in `Datum-Atlas/docs/PLAN-0.3.0.md` §6 (0.3.0 scope, use phase, cost model, the process list, how data is checked, whether 0.2 works on a real PC).
- **Next:** unblock it; make data verification its main job.

## Priorities (in order)

1. **Apps in use (Plan, Plot, Calc):** user-reported issues come before any new feature.
2. **Atlas:** answer its six questions; make data verification its main work.
3. **Sim:** restart with 0.3 control design.
4. **Pipe:** validate against the user's past projects, then dynamics.
5. **Schema:** harden (real drawings, a DEXPI file from another program, a 100-sheet project) before more features.
6. **Calc:** matrices, then programming.
7. **Plan and Plot:** finish 1.0 (signed installer, Plot's validation report, Plan's exchange-file tests, guides).
8. **Mesh:** CAD geometry and meshing of real parts; tested on real mesher files.

## For the whole family

- **A real-PC test week after the exhibition:** every app on a clean Windows PC with one checklist (install, every example, print, export, update), with findings sent to each app's chat.
- **One code-signing certificate** for all eight installers (removes the Windows warning; the most visible improvement for visitors).
- **An issue loop from real users:** an easy place for users to report problems (GitHub issues per app), read by each app's chat before it adds features.
